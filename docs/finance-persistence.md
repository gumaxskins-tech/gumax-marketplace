# Finance persistence

Completed: ledger, hold, order, payment and refund idempotency extraction, plus payment event/webhook deduplication, each behind an explicit in-memory adapter for tests. P0-A1.1a is complete: all six idempotency/deduplication mechanisms are decoupled from FinanceService. LedgerRepository, WalletRepository, HoldRepository, OrderRepository, PaymentRepository, RefundRepository and InventoryReservationRepository are also extracted and currently use in-memory adapters; they are not PostgreSQL persistence. Wallet balance remains derived from the ledger. P0-A1.1b is complete only in the sense that main finance and inventory-reservation storage is behind repository ports. FinanceTransactionContext and InMemoryFinanceTransactionManager now provide an async composition boundary with one shared set of finance dependencies. The six idempotency/dedup ports and their in-memory adapters are asynchronous; FinanceService awaits every affected store call. LedgerRepository, WalletRepository and their in-memory adapters are also asynchronous; wallet creation, balance, available balance, post, credit, debit, hold, capture, and ledger reads await the relevant ports. Wallet still has no authoritative balance field.

Pending: conversion of HoldRepository, OrderRepository, PaymentRepository, RefundRepository and InventoryReservationRepository, plus their remaining FinanceService operations, to async; persistent idempotency adapters with database scope/key semantics; PrismaTransactionManager; Prisma adapters; PostgreSQL transactions; row-level locking or conditional reservation; atomic order/reservation, payment/order/event-dedup/audit and hold/ledger-capture flows; Audit persistence; and persistent wallet/ledger/payment/refund state. InMemoryFinanceTransactionManager is a composition boundary only: it shares context and propagates errors but provides no rollback or production atomicity. RefundRepository is in-memory only; refunds still have no ledger effect, and refund/payment/audit are not a PostgreSQL transaction. The item-only reservation model has no ownership-based foreign-release validation or FinanceService release flow. In-memory reservation exclusivity applies only to a shared adapter in one process. P0-A1 is not complete.
